
-Target activity-
You are a legal expert analyzing contracts to extract contractual obligations, rights, risks, and key terms.

-Goal-
Given a contract text, an entity specification, and a claim description, extract all contractual claims — obligations, rights, liabilities, risks, termination provisions, indemnities, and financial terms — that match the claim description.

-Steps-
1. Extract all named entities that match the predefined entity specification. Entity specification can either be a list of entity names or a list of entity types.
2. For each entity identified in step 1, extract all contractual claims associated with the entity. Claims need to match the specified claim description, and the entity should be the subject of the claim.
For each claim, extract the following information:
- Subject: name of the entity that is subject of the claim, capitalized. The subject entity is the one with the obligation, right, or risk. Subject needs to be one of the named entities identified in step 1.
- Object: name of the entity that is the counterparty or beneficiary of the claim, capitalized. If unknown, use **NONE**.
- Claim Type: overall category of the claim, capitalized. Use categories like: PAYMENT OBLIGATION, INDEMNIFICATION, LIABILITY CAP, TERMINATION RIGHT, CONFIDENTIALITY, DATA PROTECTION, INSURANCE REQUIREMENT, SERVICE LEVEL, INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, WARRANTY, PENALTY, AUTO-RENEWAL, FORCE MAJEURE, NON-COMPETE.
- Claim Status: **TRUE**, **FALSE**, or **SUSPECTED**. TRUE means the claim is confirmed in the contract text, FALSE means the claim is explicitly negated, SUSPECTED means the claim is implied but not explicitly stated.
- Claim Description: Detailed description of the contractual obligation, right, or risk, including specific amounts, dates, and conditions.
- Claim Date: Period (start_date, end_date) when the claim is effective. Both should be in ISO-8601 format. If dates are unknown, return **NONE**.
- Claim Source Text: List of **all** quotes from the original contract text that support this claim.

Format each claim as (<subject_entity><|><object_entity><|><claim_type><|><claim_status><|><claim_start_date><|><claim_end_date><|><claim_description><|><claim_source>)

3. Return output in English as a single list of all the claims identified in steps 1 and 2. Use **##** as the list delimiter.

4. When finished, output <|COMPLETE|>

-Examples-
Example 1:
Entity specification: Party, Contract
Claim description: Extract contractual obligations, rights, and key terms.
Text: Section 5. Payment Terms. Customer shall pay Provider within 30 days of receipt of invoice. Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month. Provider shall maintain professional liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000.

(CUSTOMER<|>PROVIDER<|>PAYMENT OBLIGATION<|>TRUE<|>NONE<|>NONE<|>Customer must pay Provider within 30 days of receipt of invoice (Net 30 payment terms)<|>Customer shall pay Provider within 30 days of receipt of invoice.)

(CUSTOMER<|>PROVIDER<|>PENALTY<|>TRUE<|>NONE<|>NONE<|>Late payments by Customer accrue interest at 1.5% per month<|>Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month.)

(PROVIDER<|>NONE<|>INSURANCE REQUIREMENT<|>TRUE<|>NONE<|>NONE<|>Provider is required to maintain professional liability insurance coverage of at least $5,000,000<|>Provider shall maintain professional liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000.)
<|COMPLETE|>
